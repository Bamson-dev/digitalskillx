AI Ads Automation Masterclass: quiz answer notes
Attach this file to the host lesson of each quiz. The LMS shows the correct option after a pass but has no explanation field.

Quiz 1: Objectives and conversion tracking

Q1. You sell a ₦14,999 online course and your payment confirmation page can send a Purchase event. Which optimisation event is the best starting choice?
Correct answer: Purchase, or an earlier event if you cannot get enough Purchase events
Why: Meta learns from the event you choose. Choose the one closest to the real result you want, and step back to an earlier event only when volume is too low.

Q2. At which level do you choose the image, video and text a person sees?
Correct answer: Ad
Why: The ad level holds the creative, copy and destination.

Q3. Why test your tracking events before spending real budget?
Correct answer: To confirm each event fires once, with the right value and currency, so Meta learns from correct data
Why: Wrong or duplicated events teach the system the wrong thing.

Q4. A campaign optimised for link clicks brings many visits and no buyers. What is the most likely reason?
Correct answer: The system found people likely to click, not people likely to buy
Why: The system optimises toward the event you chose.

Q5. The browser pixel and a server event both report the same purchase. What lets Meta count it once?
Correct answer: The same event ID on both
Why: A shared event ID is the deduplication key.

Quiz 2: Research, angles, copy and creatives

Q1. You generated 30 hooks with AI. What is the best next step?
Correct answer: Select a few using written criteria, then check claims and policy
Why: AI generates options. You choose and verify.

Q2. An AI draft says 'Guaranteed to double your sales.' What should you do?
Correct answer: Remove it, because you cannot prove it and it can break advertising rules
Why: Do not promise results you cannot prove.

Q3. Which proof can you use in an ad?
Correct answer: A real customer message you have permission to use
Why: Use only proof you actually have.

Q4. Your AI generated product image shows a distorted label. What do you do?
Correct answer: Fix it, regenerate it, or use a real photo
Why: Check every AI image before it goes live.

Q5. Your audience does not yet know they have the problem. What should the opening do?
Correct answer: Describe a situation they recognise from their own day
Why: Unaware audiences respond to recognition, not product detail.

Quiz 3: Landing pages and tracking

Q1. What does the Test Events tool let you do?
Correct answer: See events from your browser arriving in real time
Why: It is the fastest way to confirm an event reaches Meta.

Q2. What value should a Purchase event carry?
Correct answer: The real amount paid, with currency NGN
Why: Value and currency should match the actual payment.

Q3. Your thank-you page fires Purchase again each time it is refreshed. What is the risk?
Correct answer: Purchases are counted more than once, which distorts your numbers
Why: Fire the event once per payment reference.

Q4. If you send customer email to Meta through server events, how must it be sent?
Correct answer: Normalised and hashed, never plain text
Why: Hash customer identifiers before sending.

Q5. Your page takes about nine seconds to load on mobile data. What is the likely effect?
Correct answer: Many visitors leave before the page loads
Why: Compress images and simplify the page.

Quiz 4: Budgets, tests and metrics

Q1. Click through rate is best described as:
Correct answer: Clicks divided by impressions
Why: It shows how often people who saw the ad clicked.

Q2. CPM measures:
Correct answer: Cost per 1,000 impressions
Why: CPM is the cost of 1,000 impressions.

Q3. CPC is high and CTR is low. Where should you look first?
Correct answer: The hook, creative or audience
Why: Low CTR points to the ad itself or who sees it.

Q4. Spend is ₦30,000 and you recorded 10 sales at ₦14,999 each. What is the cost per purchase?
Correct answer: ₦3,000
Why: ₦30,000 divided by 10 equals ₦3,000.

Q5. With the same figures, what is the return on ad spend before other costs?
Correct answer: About 5
Why: Revenue ₦149,990 divided by spend ₦30,000 is about 5.0. This ignores product and other costs.

Quiz 5: Diagnosis, scaling and compliance

Q1. Which set of signals best supports a decision to scale?
Correct answer: Several stable days, positive unit economics and verified tracking
Why: Scale on evidence, not on one result.

Q2. Which approach is safer when increasing budget?
Correct answer: Gradual increases with a stop rule
Why: Large jumps can disturb delivery. Increase in steps and record each one.

Q3. An ad is rejected. What is the best first step?
Correct answer: Read the reason, compare it with the current policy, then edit or appeal
Why: Understand the reason before acting.

Q4. Which item must you never paste into an AI assistant?
Correct answer: Access tokens, passwords or private customer data
Why: Keep secrets and private data out of AI tools.

Q5. You duplicate an ad set to test a new audience. What is one risk?
Correct answer: Audience overlap and a split budget
Why: Overlapping audiences can compete and split spend.
