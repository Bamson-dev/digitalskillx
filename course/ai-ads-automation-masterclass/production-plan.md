1. Workload estimate

Finished teaching time: 620 minutes (10 h 20 min) across 41 lessons.
Lessons by format: 19 screen recordings (305 min), 14 mixed slides plus screen (203 min), 8 slides with narration only (112 min).
Lessons that need screen recordings or live demos: 33 of 41, listed in section 2.
These are planning estimates, not measured figures. Change the multipliers after you record the first two lessons.
Assumptions: recording and retakes 1.5 hours per finished hour (about 15.5 h), editing 3 hours per finished hour (about 31 h), slide preparation 45 minutes per slide lesson for 22 lessons (about 16.5 h). Total about 63 hours of your time, plus time to upload and build quizzes (about 6 h) and write cover and storefront copy (about 2 h).
Suggested schedule: record one module per day, Modules 1 to 3 first so you can test the upload flow early.

5. Storefront copy

Short description
Learn how to use AI to plan, write, launch and review Facebook and Instagram ad campaigns for your Nigerian business, with worksheets, templates and a practical campaign project.

Full description
This course shows you how to use AI tools to cut the manual work in Meta Ads Manager and make clearer decisions. You will work through 41 lessons in 16 modules, from account setup and conversion tracking to copywriting, creatives, landing pages, testing, reading metrics and scaling. Every module ends with a practical task for your own business. You also get a prompt library, ad copy templates, a creative brief, planning and research worksheets, a performance tracker, and launch and optimisation checklists.
Examples use Naira prices and common Nigerian business situations. Examples are invented to teach the method. They are not results from real businesses. Advertising results depend on your offer, price, audience, creative, tracking and budget. This course does not promise sales, income or any specific result.

Learning outcomes
After this course you will be able to:
- Explain what AI can and cannot do in Meta advertising.
- Set up Meta Business Suite, Ads Manager and a Pixel, and test your tracking.
- Choose a campaign objective and optimisation event that fits your goal.
- Research customers and build a list of advertising angles with AI, and score them.
- Write and edit headlines, hooks and ad copy that stay accurate and policy aware.
- Brief and produce ad creatives with AI tools.
- Plan a landing page that matches your ad.
- Build a campaign with a test budget based on your break even numbers.
- Read the main metrics and diagnose weak performance step by step.
- Decide when to change, pause or scale a campaign using written rules.
- Plan, launch and review one complete campaign.

Prerequisites
- A smartphone or computer with reliable internet.
- A product or service you can sell, or a client business you can practise on.
- A Facebook profile. A Page and Instagram account are set up in Module 3.
- A payment method that Meta accepts for your account (a live campaign needs it, the project can be drafted without it).
- No design or coding skills needed.
