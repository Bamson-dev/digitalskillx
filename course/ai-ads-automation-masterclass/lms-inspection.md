The unverified items from the first pass are now resolved as follows.
Maximum video upload size: the LMS has no video upload. Attachments and resources are limited to 25 MB.
Accepted video formats: none uploaded. Embeds from YouTube, Vimeo, Wistia, Loom, or any direct file URL the browser can play.
Quiz explanations: not supported. Use quiz-answer-notes.txt.
Short answer marking: manual, and it blocks auto completion. Quizzes now use multiple choice only.
Retake limits: supported on the quiz page only.
Certificate trigger: completion percentage plus all quizzes passed, automatic when the Certificate setting is on.
Notifications: publishing emails all students on the platform with any enrolment or paid transaction. Assignment publishing emails enrolled students.
